# AI Interview Coach

You are helping me build an AI Mock Interview app.

 

Write the system prompt my app will use. The AI

interviewer must:

1. Ask exactly 5 questions for the chosen role,

   one at a time (never all at once)

2. Wait for my answer before the next question

3. After question 5 — give a score out of 10 for

   each answer, 2 strengths, 2 weaknesses, and

   one model answer for the weakest response

4. Keep a professional but encouraging tone

 

Also give me 3 role presets: SDE Intern,

Data Analyst, Frontend Developer.
